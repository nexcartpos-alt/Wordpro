Purpose

All the tools you need for
everyday writing

Word is heavy, and simpler editors don’t feel familiar enough to stay in flow. That’s why I built word-pro.

It keeps the layout you already know, the ribbon, the page and the status bar, and drops everything else. One Java file, no external libraries, and it starts instantly.

Because it’s a single readable file, you can open it up and change any feature to fit your needs.

  • ¶Document EditingA page on a grey canvas with undo and redo, cut, copy and paste, find and replace-all, select all, a live word and character count, and keyboard shortcuts for save, find, bold, italic and underline.
  • BText FormattingFont family and size, bold, italic, underline and strikethrough, font colour and highlight colour, with formatting that follows the selection or the next characters you type.
  • HStyles & ParagraphsNormal and Heading 1 to 3 styles, left, centre, right and justified alignment, indent and outdent, line spacing from 1.0 to 2.0, and bulleted and numbered lists you can toggle on and off.
  • +InsertPictures from disk (large images are scaled to fit), a horizontal line, today’s date and page-break markers.
  • ▯Page LayoutNarrow, normal and wide margins, portrait and landscape page shapes, and a dark canvas option under the View tab.
  • ▤FilesNew, open and save as RTF (opens in Word and most editors), open and save plain text, and print from the File menu.
  • ΩSymbols for AssignmentsA symbol picker (Ctrl+M) with 270+ Unicode math and text symbols: Greek, operators, relations, sets and logic, arrows, super and subscripts, fractions and currency. Type \alpha then space and it turns into the symbol, and ready-made equations insert in one click.
  • ▦Real PagesA true page view with a gap between pages, Letter, A4, Legal and A5 paper, margins, landscape, and page breaks that start a new page.
  • ▣Pictures & Image EditingPaste pictures straight from the browser or a screenshot with Ctrl+V. Double-click a picture to crop, rotate, flip and adjust brightness, contrast, grayscale, sepia or invert. Right-click for size, position and save options.
  • JTechnologyPure Java Swing with no external libraries. Download a ready-made app for Windows, macOS or Linux from the Releases page, or build it yourself with ./build.sh.

Good to know. word-pro is a lightweight editor. Pictures are kept when you export to HTML but are not stored in RTF files yet. Tables, hyperlinks, zoom, headers and footers, and native .docx import and export are not included yet.
